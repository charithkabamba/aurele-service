Our Environment

Committed to Sustainable Smelting Practices

At Lualaba Copper Smelter, we are deeply committed to protecting the environment through sustainable smelting practices. We continuously strive to minimize our environmental impact by implementing eco-friendly technologies and adhering to stringent environmental regulations. Our goal is to ensure that our operations not only meet but exceed industry standards for environmental stewardship.

Eco-Friendly Technologies

We invest in cutting-edge technologies that reduce emissions and waste, promoting a cleaner and healthier environment.

Waste Management

Our comprehensive waste management strategies ensure that we minimize environmental impact while maximizing resource recovery.

Community Engagement

We actively engage with local communities to promote environmental awareness and support sustainable development initiatives.
