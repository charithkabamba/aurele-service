Safety First at Lualaba Copper Smelter

Committed to a Safe and Secure Smelting Environment

At Lualaba Copper Smelter, safety is our top priority. We are dedicated to maintaining a safe working environment for our employees, clients, and the communities we serve. Our comprehensive safety protocols and training programs ensure that we adhere to the highest industry standards, minimizing risks and promoting a culture of safety throughout our operations.

Comprehensive Safety Training

Our employees undergo rigorous safety training to ensure they are well-equipped to handle any situation that may arise in the smelting process.

State-of-the-Art Safety Equipment

We utilize the latest safety equipment and technology to protect our workers and ensure safe smelting operations.

Regular Safety Audits

We conduct regular safety audits and inspections to identify potential hazards and implement corrective measures promptly.
