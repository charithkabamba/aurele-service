Our Community

Building Stronger Communities Together

At Lualaba Copper Smelter, we believe in the power of community and are dedicated to fostering strong relationships with the people and regions we serve. Our commitment to social responsibility drives us to engage in initiatives that promote education, health, and economic development, ensuring a positive impact on the communities around us.

Educational Programs

We support local schools and educational initiatives to empower the next generation with knowledge and skills for a brighter future.

Health Initiatives

Our health programs focus on improving access to medical services and promoting wellness in the communities we operate.

Economic Development

We invest in local businesses and infrastructure projects that create jobs and stimulate economic growth.
